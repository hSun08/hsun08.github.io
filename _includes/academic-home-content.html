I’m Haixiang Sun, a Ph.D. student in the Edwardson School of Industrial Engineering at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Andrew Liu. I work at the intersection of machine learning and optimization to make decisions more reliable under uncertainty.

My research brings the structure of optimization into learning systems, spanning differentiable optimization, optimal transport, and scalable machine learning, with applications in energy scheduling and beyond.

A question that drives my work
